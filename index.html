About JRG

Built by hand. Backed by pride.

JRG Construction & Remodeling Inc. is an owner-operated construction company led by Jaime R. Gomez. Jaime is on site for every project — from the first cut to the final coat of paint — so the quality you see in these photos is the quality you'll get in your home.

We take on the full scope: framing, drywall, plumbing, electrical, tile, flooring, cabinets, stucco, concrete, fencing and stairs. That means one point of contact, one schedule, and one standard of workmanship across your entire project.
